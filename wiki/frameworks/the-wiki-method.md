# The Wiki Method

> WikiCreator's proprietary four-step process for turning accumulated expertise into a maintained reference site.

The Wiki Method is the framework WikiCreator teaches in onboarding, sells inside [[offer-architecture|Wiki-in-a-Box]], and references throughout external content. It is not the product; it is the methodology the product automates. The method is deliberately simple — four steps, no sub-steps — and is positioned against the "build a marketing site" methodology embedded in most website builders.

## The four steps

### 1. Source

Collect raw material. The audit covers recorded calls (sales, coaching, client, podcast), written long-form (LinkedIn, newsletters, internal memos), slide decks, frameworks, and voice memos. The typical 7-year operator finds 80–120 hours of usable material on first audit, of which they had previously identified less than 20%.

### 2. Structure

The AI ingests sources, classifies them by type, and produces a candidate article scaffold. Cross-references are generated automatically. The output at this stage is a draft wiki, not a finished one.

### 3. Refine

Human review. The customer (or a Wiki-in-a-Box strategist) edits voice, corrects facts, and removes anything they would not want public. ~80% of the AI output ships unedited; the remaining 20% is where the human's judgment lives.

### 4. Publish

The wiki is published to a domain or subdomain. New sources arriving after publication re-enter step 1; the wiki maintains itself from then on.

## Why four steps and not more

Methodologies with more than five steps lose adoption fast. The Wiki Method's design constraint was that a customer should be able to recall it without notes within one week of onboarding. Four steps cleared that bar in user testing; five did not.

## How the method shows up in the product

| Step | Product surface |
|---|---|
| Source | `raw/` ingest folder, source audit checklist |
| Structure | Article generator, cross-reference engine |
| Refine | Edit mode, review queue |
| Publish | Domain setup, publish button |
