# Positioning

> WikiCreator is the AI web design platform for operators who want a real site, not a template.

WikiCreator generates production-ready websites from a single brief. The platform is positioned against two ends of the market: drag-and-drop builders (Wix, Squarespace) that produce template-grade output, and code-generation tools (v0, Bolt) that require developer fluency. WikiCreator sits in the middle — a design-literate AI that produces opinionated, on-brand sites for non-technical operators in under an hour.

The brand is built on a single, repeated claim: **most AI web tools generate code; WikiCreator generates design.** This distinction is the wedge. It shows up in the product (a design system is generated before any markup), in the marketing (every demo leads with the visual output, not the prompt), and in the pricing (a flat monthly fee, not per-generation credits).

## Positioning statement

> _For small business operators and indie founders who need a credible website without hiring a designer, WikiCreator is the AI web design platform that produces opinionated, on-brand sites in under an hour — unlike template builders that look generic or code generators that require developer skills._

## Three pillars

| Pillar | Claim | Proof signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Design-first** | A design system is generated before any markup | [[design-first-generation]] framework |
| **Operator-grade** | Built for non-technical founders, not designers | 78% of users self-identify as non-technical |
| **Opinionated** | One strong direction, not ten mediocre options | First-pass acceptance rate of 64% |

## What the brand is not

- Not a template marketplace.
- Not a code generator.
- Not a multi-purpose AI assistant.
- Not aimed at agencies as the primary buyer (though agencies are a secondary segment — see [[icp-primary]]).
