# Founder story

> WikiCreator was founded by Maya Chen in 2024 after a decade of building software at Stripe and editing Wikipedia in her spare time.

Maya Chen ran the Edit Patrol team on English Wikipedia from 2014 to 2019 as a volunteer, reviewing roughly 18,000 edits over five years. She joined Stripe in 2017 as a frontend engineer and left in 2023 to build WikiCreator full-time after watching three consultant friends spend more than $40,000 between them trying to translate years of client work into a public website. None of them shipped.

The original prototype, built in 2024, was a script that ingested call transcripts and produced linked markdown articles. Maya tested it on her own consulting notes from her side practice and produced a 60-article site in two weeks. She showed it to the same three friends. All three signed up. The waitlist hit 900 within six weeks.

## Why this matters for the brand

Maya's Wikipedia background is load-bearing for the voice. The product's editorial conventions — neutral phrasing, citation discipline, "See also" sections — come directly from the [Wikipedia Manual of Style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style), not from a marketing decision. References to "MoS" in the wiki point to the same source Maya was trained on as a volunteer editor.
