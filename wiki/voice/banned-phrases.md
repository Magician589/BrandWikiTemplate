# Banned phrases

> A maintained list of words and phrases that never appear in WikiCreator copy on owned surfaces.

This is the operational counterpart to the [[voice-guide]]. The list is kept short and binding. If a phrase is on this list and appears in a draft, the draft does not ship.

## The list

| Banned | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Revolutionary | Empty intensifier | A specific claim with a number |
| Game-changing | Empty intensifier | A specific claim with a number |
| AI-powered (as marketing adjective) | Describes the substrate, not the product | Describe what the AI does |
| Unlock | Vague metaphor | Name the specific outcome |
| Supercharge | Vague intensifier | Cite the magnitude |
| 10× | Hyperbolic and unverified | The actual multiplier, with a source |
| Synergy | Corporate filler | Cut entirely |
| Leverage (as a verb) | Filler | "Use" |
| World-class | Unverifiable | Cite the third-party benchmark |
| We are excited to announce | Hedging fluff | Lead with the announcement itself |
| Cutting-edge | Date-stamped to itself | Describe the specific capability |
| Disrupt / disruptive | Overused | Describe what changes for the customer |

## Why these specifically

These phrases have two things in common: they make claims that cannot be checked, and they pattern-match to the marketing voice WikiCreator is positioned against. Removing them is the cheapest way to keep the brand legible as a reference work rather than a vendor.

## Three-adjective rule

Any sentence with three or more adjectives in a row is also rejected. Examples that fail:

- "Fast, intuitive, beautiful workflow"
- "Powerful, flexible, modern platform"

Rewrite with one adjective and a verb.
