# Voice Guide

> Direct, design-literate, and quietly opinionated — never enthusiastic, never apologetic.

WikiCreator's voice is built around a single instruction: **sound like a senior designer giving advice over coffee, not a SaaS landing page.** The voice is short-sentenced, declarative, and confident. It avoids hype words, exclamation marks, and the language of generic productivity software. It assumes the reader is intelligent and time-poor.

The voice is also distinct from the founder's personal voice on social — that channel runs warmer and more discursive. This guide governs product copy, marketing site, onboarding, and email. See [[content-strategy]] for channel-specific tone.

## Principles

1. **Lead with the noun.** _"Sites in an hour."_ Not _"Get your website up and running in just one hour!"_
2. **Name the trade-off.** Confidence comes from acknowledging what the product doesn't do, not from claiming it does everything.
3. **Cut adverbs.** _Easily, simply, quickly, beautifully_ are banned. If the claim needs an adverb, the claim is weak.
4. **Use design vocabulary correctly.** Type scale, optical alignment, grid, hierarchy — these terms appear in copy because the audience values seeing them used well.
5. **No hedging.** _"We believe," "We think," "It might"_ → cut. State the position.

## Signature phrases

- _"Sites, not templates."_
- _"Generated, not assembled."_
- _"Design-first."_
- _"Looks like you hired someone."_
- _"Opinionated by default."_

These appear across the marketing site, product copy, and founder talks. They are load-bearing — change them deliberately and update this guide.

## Banned phrases

- _"Revolutionize," "game-changing," "AI-powered," "seamless," "effortless"_
- _"Just" as a softener_ ("just click here")
- _"Beautiful websites"_ — every competitor says this; it means nothing.
- _"Drag and drop"_ — implies template builder positioning, which WikiCreator rejects.
- _"Unleash your creativity"_ — opposite of the brand's promise (the AI is the creative; the user is the operator).

## Comparative examples

| Off-brand | On-brand |
|---|---|
| _Easily create stunning websites with AI._ | _Generate a real site in under an hour._ |
| _Our powerful platform unlocks your creativity._ | _One strong direction. Edit if you want, but most don't._ |
| _Trusted by thousands of businesses worldwide._ | _Used by 4,200 operators. See the gallery._ |
| _Sign up today to get started!_ | _Start a generation._ |

## Voice on social

The founder, Mira Vance, writes longer-form on LinkedIn and X. Her personal voice is permitted to be warmer, more anecdotal, and more self-deprecating. Brand-channel posts (the WikiCreator handles) remain in the voice described above. See [[content-strategy]].
