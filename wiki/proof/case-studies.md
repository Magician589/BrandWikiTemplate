# Case Studies

> Three short case studies covering the primary use cases: founder-led launch, agency white-label, and credibility upgrade.

WikiCreator's proof stack is deliberately narrow — three canonical case studies, each mapped to a buying trigger from [[icp-primary]]. Each is presented with the same shape: situation, what they tried first, generation experience, outcome. Short enough to be quoted in a sales conversation; specific enough to be believable.

Quotes are pulled from recorded onboarding calls and follow-up interviews. Full transcripts live in `raw/`.

## Case 1 — Finn & Co. (founder-led launch)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer | Finn & Co., 2-person fractional CMO consultancy |
| Trigger | Closing first $40k retainer; needed a credible site to send the contract from |
| Tried first | Squarespace template, then Framer (abandoned after 6 hours) |
| Time on WikiCreator | 42 minutes from prompt to publish |
| Outcome | Retainer signed; site since used to close two more deals |

> _"I'd been avoiding the website conversation for four months. WikiCreator gave me something I was actually proud to send. The client mentioned it on the kickoff call."_ — Jules R., Finn & Co.

Source: `raw/2026-04-sales-call-finn-and-co.md`

## Case 2 — Northbank Studio (agency white-label)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer | Northbank Studio, 4-person brand agency |
| Trigger | Client backlog; needed to deliver 6 small-business sites in Q1 without hiring |
| Tier | Agency ($249/mo) |
| Time per site | 2–4 hours including client review |
| Outcome | Delivered 9 sites in Q1; gross margin per site up from 38% to 71% |

> _"We charge clients the same $4,500 we always did. We just deliver in three days instead of three weeks. The clients don't know — and frankly don't need to."_ — Owen M., Northbank Studio

## Case 3 — Mira's friend Anna (credibility upgrade)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer | Anna K., independent leadership coach |
| Trigger | A prospect asked for her site; she had a Calendly link only |
| Tried first | Nothing — was avoiding the project entirely |
| Time on WikiCreator | 1 hour 10 minutes |
| Outcome | Closed the prospect; reported a 3x increase in inbound replies over 60 days |

> _"I'd been telling people I 'don't really do a website.' That stopped being a tenable position when I started charging $15k engagements. WikiCreator solved a problem I was ashamed of."_ — Anna K.
